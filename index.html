Before you go
  • Install Santander Cycles, add a card, buy a Day Pass (~£3.50, unlimited rides under an hour each). E-bikes cost ~£1 more.
  • Backup bikes: Lime (dockless). In Camden, end the ride in a marked bay.
  • Hotel → Prufrock by bike: follow the Cycleway 6 signs south, mostly a separate lane down Farringdon Road. Skip Gray's Inn Road (busy, buses).
  • Rain: walk to Prufrock instead (~25 min) or Uber.
  • Book lunch for 5.

Shopping

Shops open ~11 and most close at 7. Wed–Thu meetings end at 5:30, which leaves about 75 minutes: walk to a King's Cross or Lamb's Conduit pick. The Uber picks need a free afternoon.

Coal Drops Yard

10 min

Your densest menswear stop, 10 minutes away.

Shops 11–7 (Sun 11–5). Victorian coal sheds. Picks for your sizes and budget:

  • Universal Works: British workwear-leaning menswear. Best size bet: XXL runs roomy (one shirt measures ~52" chest).
  • Carhartt WIP: the European, fashion-cut side of Carhartt. Chore coats ~£190, trousers ~£100. Goes to XXL (~47" chest), so try jackets; W40 trousers are hit or miss. On the King's Cross estate, not in the Yard itself.
  • Beyond Retro: big vintage shop; American vintage runs large. Dig the men's rails. To 8 on Thu.
  • Cubitts: London-made frames. Acetate frames £150–175 with prescription lenses included; skip titanium (£250+).
  • Skip: MHL and A.P.C. (£200+ shirts, slim cuts), Paul Smith (pricey, slim).
  • A drink after: Vermuteria (vermouth bar) or Parrillan (Spanish grill, heated terrace).

Lamb's Conduit Street

12 min

London's best short street for independent menswear.

One short street; do all of it. Picks for your sizes: check hours

  • Oliver Spencer (the brand's first shop): British-made shirts, knits, trousers. Shirts go to a 17.5 collar, about a 51" garment chest, so they should fit.
  • Folk (no. 49, to 7): boxy, relaxed cuts that help. Tops go to XXL (their size 6); try a 6.
  • TOAST (no. 59, to 6:30): loose, workwear-ish linen and wool. Relaxed cuts; ask for the largest size.
  • The Content Store: multi-brand, so sizes vary by label. Worth a look in passing.
  • Gone: the Universal Works shop here closed. Use Coal Drops Yard or Soho.
