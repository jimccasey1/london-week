Before you go
  • Install Santander Cycles, add a card, buy a Day Pass (£3.50, unlimited rides under an hour each). E-bikes cost ~£1 more.
  • Backup bikes: Lime (dockless). In Camden, end the ride in a marked bay.
  • Hotel → Prufrock by bike: follow the Cycleway 6 signs south, mostly a separate lane down Farringdon Road. Skip Gray's Inn Road (busy, buses).
  • Rain: walk to Prufrock instead (~25 min) or Uber.
  • Book lunch for 5.

Shopping

Most shops close at 6:30 or 7. Wed–Thu meetings end at 5:30, so after work you get 45–75 minutes on foot nearby. Brick Lane works only if you go straight there.

Coal Drops Yard

walk 10 min

The nearest cluster of menswear, in Victorian coal sheds.

Shops 11–7. Picks for your sizes and budget: check

  • Universal Works: British workwear-leaning menswear. Your best size bet: XXL runs roomy (one shirt measures ~52" chest).
  • Beyond Retro (units 79–81): vintage; American pieces run large. Dig the men's rails. To 8 on Thu.
  • Cubitts: London-made frames. Acetate £150–175 with prescription lenses included; skip titanium (£250+).
  • Carhartt WIP (next door, 2 Pancras Square, 11–7, coffee bar inside): Carhartt's European fashion line. XXL is ~47" chest, likely tight on you; try boxy jackets only.
  • Skip: MHL and A.P.C. (£200+ shirts, slim cuts), Paul Smith (pricey, slim).
  • A bite after: Parrillan (Spanish grill, heated terrace).
WalkUber

Lamb's Conduit Street

walk 12 min

One short Bloomsbury street of independent menswear shops.

Most close 6:30; after meetings you get ~45 min. Picks for your sizes: check

  • Oliver Spencer (no. 58, to 6:30): British-made shirts, knits, trousers. Shirts go to a 17.5 collar (~51" garment chest). Shirts often top £150.
  • Folk (no. 49, to 7): boxy, relaxed cuts. Tops go to XXL, their size 6.
  • TOAST (no. 59, to 6:30): loose linen and wool. Ask for the largest size.
  • The Content Store: multi-brand; sizes vary by label.
  • Gone: the Universal Works shop here closed. Use Coal Drops Yard.

walk 5 min

Three secondhand and academic bookshops within a block.

Good for an odd half hour. check
